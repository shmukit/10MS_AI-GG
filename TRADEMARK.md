## Code vs. Brand

This repository is licensed under the [MIT License](LICENSE). The MIT license grants you broad rights to use, modify, and distribute the **source code**.

However, the MIT license does **not** grant rights to use the **trademarks, logos, or brand names** of 10 Minute School.

## Protected Marks

The following are trademarks or brand assets of **10 Minute School**:

- **10MS** / **10 Minute School**
- **SheSTEM**
- Logos in `src/assets/branding/` (SVG files)
- Product names and taglines used in the application UI

## What you may do

- Fork this repository and modify the code for your own organization
- Deploy a modified version for internal or educational use
- Contribute improvements back via pull requests
- Reference the project name when describing your fork's origin (e.g., "based on 10MS SheSTEM")

## What you may not do

- Use 10 Minute School or SheSTEM logos in your fork without written permission
- Imply that your deployment is officially affiliated with or endorsed by 10 Minute School
- Register domain names, social media handles, or app store listings using these marks
- Remove trademark notices from forked copies of this policy

## Replacing branding in a fork

If you deploy your own instance, replace:

1. Logos in `src/assets/branding/`
2. App name references in `src/config/environment.ts`
3. Marketing copy in `src/components/Marketing/`
4. Favicon and manifest files in `public/`
